## In production

Mãe Salvador 360º is the pregnancy and postpartum app and staff portal for the municipal maternal health program of Salvador, Brazil, launched by the city on 17 November 2026 for an expected 20,000 pregnant women per year.

As lead developer working with the health department's IT team, I built the patient app (Expo/React Native on iOS, Android, and web), served as main contributor to the API (NestJS, GraphQL, PostgreSQL) and staff portal (Next.js 16), and handle store releases, production QA, and deployment runbooks. The platform reads clinical records from e-SUS APS and integrates with municipal maternity, emergency care, and transit-card systems.
