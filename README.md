### Hi, I'm Ayan!

Physician turned engineer focused on applied AI: RAG, agents, and the infrastructure to run them.

**In production**
- **Mãe Salvador 360º**: pregnancy and postpartum app and staff portal for the municipal maternal health program of Salvador, Brazil. Launched by the city on 17 Nov 2026, with an expected reach of about 20,000 pregnant women a year. I built the app (Expo/React Native on iOS, Android and web) and co-developed the portal (Next.js 16) and the API (NestJS, GraphQL, PostgreSQL) with the health department's IT team. The API reads clinical records from e-SUS APS, the national primary-care record system. · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/id6830919689) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=br.gov.ba.salvador.saude.maesalvador360) · [Web](https://maesalvador360-saude.salvador.ba.gov.br/gestante) · [Demo with synthetic data](https://mae-salvador-mobile.vercel.app/) · [Press](https://www.bahianoticias.com.br/saude/noticia/33351-mae-salvador-360-veja-como-funciona-o-aplicativo-que-reune-servicos-de-saude-para-gestantes) · [Feb 2026 prototype](https://github.com/ayanliger/mae-salvador-mvp)
